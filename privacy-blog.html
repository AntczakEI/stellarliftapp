Privacy Policy

Clear, practical, and aligned with how the site actually works.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

Stellar Lift Blog is the training-focused content section of the Stellar Lift website and is operated under Antczak Enterprise International (Stellar Lift).

What this policy covers

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Stellar Lift Blog, why it may be collected, how it may be used, and what choices you have.

What information may be collected

  • Basic technical and usage data, such as page views, approximate location, device type, browser type, and general interaction data.
  • Information you choose to send, such as your email address and message if you contact us directly.
  • Consent and advertising-related information, including your privacy choices and signals used to control advertising and analytics behavior.

How information may be used

  • To operate and maintain the website.
  • To understand which content is useful and improve the site.
  • To respond to messages or support requests.
  • To measure site performance and traffic where permitted.
  • To support advertising, fraud prevention, and ad measurement where applicable.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for site functionality, privacy choice storage, analytics, and advertising.

Depending on your location and your choices, these may include:

  • Essential technologies needed for basic operation and consent management.
  • Analytics technologies used to understand traffic and improve content.
  • Advertising technologies used by advertising providers such as Google AdSense.

Consent choices

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, this site may show a consent message provided through a certified consent management platform.

That message may let you:

  • Consent to data use for supported purposes,
  • Decline consent,
  • Manage options in more detail.

You can also reopen your privacy choices from the site footer using the Privacy and cookie settings link when available.

Privacy and cookie settings

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and which pages or articles are useful. Analytics behavior may depend on your location and consent choices.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising.

  • Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region and your consent choices.
  • Advertising services may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and limiting repeated ads.
  • Where required, advertising-related processing is controlled through the consent choices you make on the site.

Third-party services

Some services used on this site are provided by third parties, which may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Analytics – analytics and measurement.
  • Google AdSense – advertising, reporting, and fraud prevention.
  • GitHub Pages – site hosting and delivery.

Your choices

  • Use the site's consent interface, where shown, to manage advertising and analytics choices.
  • Reopen your choices through the Privacy and cookie settings link when available.
  • Use your browser settings to block, limit, or delete cookies.
  • You may also manage some advertising preferences through your Google account, where applicable.

Data retention

Data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for site operation, analytics, security, legal compliance, and support purposes.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact us through the Contact page or by email at office@antczak.co.uk.

Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time if the site, tools, legal requirements, or consent setup changes. The latest version will always appear on this page.
