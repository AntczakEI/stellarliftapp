office@antczak.co.uk

What to include

Tell us which app or website feature you are contacting us about, your device type where relevant, and a short description of the issue. You can include the steps that led to the problem or any error message you received.

Please do not include passwords or payment card details in your initial message.

What can we help with?

Common reasons to contact Stellar Lift support.

  • Questions about using Training Tracker or Water Tracker.
  • Problems with workout or hydration logging.
  • App availability and supported devices.
  • Questions about account access or account deletion.
  • Privacy-related questions or data requests.

  • Suggestions or feedback about current Stellar Lift products.
