Water Tracker — Drink Reminder

A simple hydration app for people who want quick water logging, practical reminders, visible daily goals, and an easier way to stay aware of their intake.

Water Tracker is designed to keep hydration tracking simple. Log drinks quickly, use reminders when they help, and keep your daily water intake visible without turning hydration into another complicated task.

Stellar Lift is primarily focused on training. Water Tracker remains available as an optional tool for people who find hydration tracking useful alongside their training or everyday routine.

What Water Tracker is for

A lightweight way to keep water intake visible without making a simple habit harder than it needs to be.

  • Logging water intake quickly during the day.
  • Using reminders when you tend to forget to drink.
  • Keeping a daily water goal visible.
  • Building a more consistent hydration routine.
  • Tracking intake without a feature-heavy wellness platform.

Why Water Tracker stays simple

Hydration tracking should be quick enough that you can actually keep doing it.

Quick logging

The app is designed to keep recording drinks fast enough to fit naturally into your day.

Practical reminders

Reminders can help when you genuinely forget to drink, without making hydration feel like a constant task.

Visible daily goals

Keeping your intake and target visible makes it easier to understand where you are during the day.

Less clutter

Water Tracker focuses on hydration rather than trying to become a full health or fitness platform.

Hydration and training

Daily water tracking and training hydration are related, but they are not exactly the same thing.

Water Tracker can help you keep everyday fluid intake visible. For harder training sessions, hydration decisions may also depend on session duration, sweat rate, heat, electrolytes, and sodium loss.

Stellar Lift covers those topics separately in the Hydration for Training section so you can go beyond a generic daily water target when training demands more specific decisions.
