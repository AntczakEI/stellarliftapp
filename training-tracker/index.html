Stellar Lift Training Tracker

Simple workout logging built around real training.

Training Tracker is the main Stellar Lift app, designed for people who want a cleaner and faster way to record workouts, organise routines, and keep a useful training history without getting buried in unnecessary features.

Training should be the hard part. Logging it should not be. Training Tracker is designed to reduce the friction between finishing a set and recording what you actually did.

The aim is simple: keep enough useful information to see your training, follow your routines, and track progress over time without turning every session into admin work.

What Training Tracker is for

A focused workout log for people who want useful records without an overloaded training app.

  • Logging workouts quickly and clearly.
  • Keeping exercises, sets, reps, and sessions organised.
  • Following routines without rebuilding your training every time.
  • Maintaining a useful history of the work you have completed.
  • Supporting consistency without adding unnecessary friction.
  • Keeping workout tracking useful enough to remain part of your training routine.

Why Training Tracker stays focused

The priority is the training workflow rather than adding features simply because another fitness app has them.

Fast by design

Workout logging should take seconds where possible. The app is designed so tracking supports the session instead of constantly interrupting it.

Less clutter

Training Tracker avoids turning straightforward workout logging into a maze of menus, dashboards, and unnecessary setup.

Useful training history

The point of recording training is to have information you can actually use later, rather than collecting data simply because the app allows it.

Built around training

Training Tracker is part of the same training-first direction as the Stellar Lift guides on programming, strength, conditioning, exercises, recovery, and performance.

Who Training Tracker is for

A practical option for people who want to train and log the work without spending unnecessary time managing the app.

Strength and gym training

Use it to record exercises, sets, reps, routines, and regular strength sessions while keeping the workflow simple.

Muay Thai and combat-sport training

Useful when gym work, conditioning, and combat-sport sessions need to fit into the same wider training week.

Consistency-focused users

A good fit for people who want enough structure to stay organised without turning training into constant data entry.

How Training Tracker fits into Stellar Lift

The app and the training content are designed to support the same process.

Learn, train, track

Stellar Lift training guides help with programming, exercise selection, strength, conditioning, weekly structure, and progression. Training Tracker gives you a place to record the sessions where those ideas are put into practice.

Hydration and recovery content support that training where useful, helping you understand factors such as fluid needs, rest, soreness, mobility, and training load.

Workout tracking should help the next session

A useful log is not just a record of what happened. It gives you context for what to do next.

Recording exercises, sets, reps, and completed sessions can help you remember previous work, follow progression more deliberately, and avoid relying on memory from one training week to the next.
