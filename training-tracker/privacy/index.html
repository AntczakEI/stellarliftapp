Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Antczak Enterprise International – Tomasz Antczak (“we”, “our”, or “us”) accesses, collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use the Stellar Lift Training Tracker mobile application (“the App”).

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

The App does not require users to create an account.

1. Information You Provide

The App does not ask you to provide your name, home address, phone number, or contact list.

If you contact us by email, we receive:

  • your email address;
  • the content of your message;
  • any information or attachments you voluntarily include.

We use this information only to:

  • respond to your enquiry;
  • provide customer support;
  • investigate technical problems;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

2. Training and App Data

Information entered into the App may include:

  • workout records;
  • exercise names;
  • sets, repetitions, weights, duration, distance, or other training values;
  • training notes;
  • training goals;
  • workout plans or routines;
  • rest timer settings;
  • reminder settings and reminder times;
  • Premium subscription status;
  • other settings selected within the App.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device.

We do not sell your workout records or use them to identify you for advertising purposes.

You can remove locally stored App data by:

  • using an available reset or deletion option in the App;
  • clearing the App’s storage in Android settings;
  • uninstalling the App.

Files exported by you, including reports or backups where available, remain in the location where you saved or shared them until you delete them yourself.

The App uses third-party services that may automatically process technical information, including:

  • device type and model;

  • IP address;
  • advertising identifier or similar device identifiers, where permitted;
  • App installation identifiers;
  • App usage and interaction information;

  • diagnostic and technical error information.

This information may be used to:

  • provide and operate the App;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • improve stability and performance;
  • understand how the App is used;
  • measure advertising performance;
  • prevent fraud and abuse;
  • maintain the security and reliability of the App.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

This information may be used for:

  • displaying contextual advertisements;

  • measuring advertising performance;
  • detecting fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising traffic.

Depending on the available options, users may:

  • review or change previous privacy choices.

You can also manage advertising personalisation through your Google account or Android device settings.

More information about how Google uses information for advertising is available at:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

These services may process:

  • App installation identifiers;

We use this information to:

  • understand how the App is used;

Premium subscriptions or in-app purchases, where available, are processed through Google Play.

We do not receive or store:

  • your full payment card number;
  • your banking details;
  • your Google account password;
  • your complete payment credentials.

Google Play may provide the App with limited information required to verify and manage Premium access, including:

  • purchased product identifier;
  • subscription entitlement status;
  • purchase token;
  • purchase acknowledgement status.

This information is used only to:

  • provide Premium functionality;
  • verify whether a subscription or purchase is active;
  • restore previous purchases;
  • manage access to Premium features.

Google’s Privacy Policy applies to information processed by Google Play:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Google Play account.

7. Notifications

The App may request permission to send workout reminders, training reminders, timer alerts, or other fitness-related notifications.

Reminder preferences and schedules may be stored locally on your device and used to display notifications at the times selected by you.

You can disable notifications:

  • through your Android device settings.

Disabling notifications does not automatically delete your other training data.

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

Workout records, exercise information, training goals, workout plans, reminder settings, timer settings, and other locally stored App information remain on your device until you:

  • delete or reset the data;
  • clear the App’s storage;
  • uninstall the App.

Exported files

Exported files, reports, or backups, where available, remain in the location selected by you until you delete them.

We do not control copies of files that you save to external storage, send to another person, or upload to another service.

Premium and purchase information

Premium entitlement information may be retained for as long as necessary to verify and provide access to Premium functionality.

Purchase and subscription records processed by Google Play are retained by Google according to Google’s own policies and applicable legal requirements.

Support correspondence

Support emails and related correspondence may be retained for up to 24 months after the support request is resolved.

They may be retained for longer where necessary to:

  • comply with legal obligations;
  • resolve disputes;
  • prevent fraud;
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Analytics, advertising, consent, and diagnostic data

Analytics, advertising, consent, crash-reporting, and diagnostic information is retained according to:

  • our service configuration;
  • the retention settings available in the relevant service;
  • the retention practices of the applicable service provider.

We do not retain a separate permanent copy of this information unless it is necessary for security, technical support, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Deletion requests

Where information is under our direct control, we will delete or anonymise it within 30 days after receiving and verifying a valid deletion request, unless a longer retention period is required for:

  • legal compliance;
  • fraud prevention;
  • security;
  • accounting or tax obligations;
  • dispute resolution;
  • the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

Some information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups, security logs, or technical systems until it is securely overwritten or deleted through the normal system lifecycle.

Because the App does not require an account, users can delete locally stored App information by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.
