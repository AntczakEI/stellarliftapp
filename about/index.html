About Stellar Lift

Built around training.

Stellar Lift focuses on practical training for people who want more structure without unnecessary complexity — with particular emphasis on Muay Thai, strength, conditioning, programming, and consistent progress.

Training is the centre of Stellar Lift. The goal is to make useful training information easier to understand and easier to apply, while providing a simple way to record the work through Training Tracker.

Hydration and recovery are covered because they affect the quality, consistency, and sustainability of training. They support the main purpose of the site rather than acting as separate directions for the brand.

The idea behind Stellar Lift

Useful training information and simple tools should help you train more consistently, not create more admin.

Training comes first

The core of Stellar Lift is practical training content: Muay Thai, strength, conditioning, workout structure, programming, exercise selection, and progression.

Practical over complicated

Training advice should help you make better decisions in real sessions. The aim is clarity and usefulness rather than making straightforward ideas sound more complex.

Consistency matters

A good training plan only works if you can follow it. Stellar Lift focuses on structures, tools, and habits that are realistic enough to keep using over time.

Tracking should stay simple

Recording workouts should not become a second workout. Training Tracker is designed to keep logging fast enough to remain useful without turning each session into admin.

Training Tracker

The main Stellar Lift app is built to support the training that the rest of the site helps you plan and understand.

Track the work without adding friction

Training Tracker provides a focused way to log workouts, organise routines, record exercises, sets and reps, and keep a useful history of your training.

The point is not to add more screens, more setup, or more complexity. It is to make the important parts of workout logging quick enough to use consistently.

What Stellar Lift tries to avoid

The same principles apply to both the content and the tools.

  • Training advice padded with generic fitness language instead of information you can use.
  • Unnecessary complexity that makes straightforward training decisions harder to understand.
  • Feature overload that makes simple workout logging slower than it needs to be.
  • Disconnected content that has little relationship to training or performance.
  • Tracking for the sake of tracking rather than recording information that is useful for future sessions.
