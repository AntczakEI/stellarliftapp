Who operates this site

Stellar Lift Blog operates under the Stellar Lift brand, part of Antczak Enterprise International.

Stellar Lift focuses on practical training content, particularly Muay Thai, strength, conditioning, workout programming, and consistent progress. Hydration and recovery guides support that training-focused approach.

This page provides a direct contact route for readers who have questions, corrections, or enquiries related to the website and its content.

What you can contact us about

  • Corrections, inaccuracies, or factual issues in a published article.
  • Questions about training guides, strength, conditioning, or workout programming content.
  • Feedback on hydration and recovery articles that support training and performance.
  • Suggestions for new articles or useful training topics.
  • Privacy-related requests or concerns.
  • Business, collaboration, or partnership enquiries.
  • General feedback about Stellar Lift Blog or the website.

How to contact us

Email: office@antczak.co.uk

If your message concerns a particular article or page, please include its direct URL. This helps us identify the content and review your question or correction more efficiently.

What to include in your message

  • A direct link to the article, page, or section you are referring to.
  • A brief explanation of your question, correction, suggestion, or request.
  • Relevant screenshots or supporting context where useful.
  • For privacy-related enquiries, enough information to identify the relevant service or concern.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive personal information in your message.

App and technical support

If your enquiry concerns Stellar Lift Training Tracker, Water Tracker, account access, account deletion, or a technical issue with an app, you can also use our dedicated support page.

Support remains available for both applications, while Training Tracker is the main app associated with Stellar Lift's training focus.
